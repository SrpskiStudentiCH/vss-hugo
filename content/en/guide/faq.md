## How do I join?

Fill out the form on the Join Us page and follow the organisation's upcoming events.

## Can I volunteer?

Yes. Volunteers are an important part of the organisation and often help with events, content and communications.

## Is there a membership fee?

The membership model can vary, but the goal is to keep joining the community simple and affordable.
