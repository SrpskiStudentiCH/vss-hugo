## Residence Permit

To study in Switzerland you need a residence permit, and in some cases a visa. Useful information:

- https://www.eda.admin.ch/countries/serbia/de/home/visa/aufenthalt/studieren.html
- https://www.eda.admin.ch/eda/de/home/einreise-aufenthalt-schweiz/visabestimmungen-visumantragsformular.html

## Funding

The scholarship system is organised at cantonal level and differs from canton to canton. We recommend checking with your university first.

- https://www.studienfinanzierung.uzh.ch/de.html
- https://ethz.ch/studierende/de/studium/finanzielles/stipendien.html
- https://www.unisg.ch/de/studium/informationsangebote/studiumfinanzieren/darlehenundstipendien

## Working While Studying

During the semester it is often possible to work up to 15 hours per week, while full-time work is possible during breaks. Students from outside the EU/EFTA need an additional work permit.

- https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/de/home/ueberuns/kontakt/kantonale_behoerden/adressen_kantone_und.html

## Housing

Students most often live in a shared flat (WG) with a shared kitchen and bathroom. Prices vary, but roughly:

- room in a WG: CHF 350–900 per month
- subsidised studio/small flat: CHF 650–1000 per month

Useful services:

- http://www.woko.ch/
- https://www.juwo.ch/
- https://www.wgzimmer.ch/wgzimmer/search/mate.html

## Costs and Transport

Include insurance, rent, food and public transport in your calculation. For transport, the SBB Student GA, ZVV Netzpass and Halbtax are useful.

## Life and Free Time

Switzerland offers excellent infrastructure, mountains, lakes and quality city life. Zurich, Uetliberg and the lakes are part of the experience that makes studying here special.
