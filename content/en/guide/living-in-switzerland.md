## What You Need to Know Right Away

This page is a practical layer for students coming to Switzerland for the first time who want to know how the city rhythm, budget and student routine work.

## Cost of Living

Typical costs are:

- health insurance
- rent and deposit
- food and basic supplies
- public transport
- books and academic materials

## Transport

Switzerland has one of the best public transport systems. Useful options:

- SBB student GA
- ZVV Netzpass
- Halbtax

## Free Time

A balance between study and life is especially important for students. Zurich, Uetliberg, the lakes and winter sports are part of the experience worth exploring.

## Advice for Freshmen

Don't try to solve everything in the first days. First secure housing, a residence permit, transport and a basic budget, then build your routine.
