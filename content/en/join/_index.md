## Membership Benefits

**Network Access**
- Connect with 120+ students, professionals and alumni
- Attend exclusive networking events
- Access member-only Slack community

**Career Support**
- Job opportunities and internship placements
- Career mentoring from experienced professionals
- Interview preparation support

**Educational Resources**
- Comprehensive guides on studying in Switzerland
- Practical information about Swiss universities
- Tips on living and adapting to Swiss culture

**Social & Cultural**
- Monthly social events
- Cultural celebrations and activities
- Sports and leisure activities

**Professional Development**
- Workshops and seminars
- Skills training sessions
- Leadership opportunities

## How to Join

1. **Fill Out Application** - Tell us about yourself and your goals
2. **Meet the Team** - Connect with current members
3. **Get Approved** - Membership reviewed by board
4. **Start Participating** - Access all member benefits

Membership is open to Serbian students and young professionals studying or working in Switzerland.

## Annual Membership: Free
We believe in keeping membership accessible. Our community is funded by partnerships and events.
