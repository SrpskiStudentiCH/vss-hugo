## Beginnings

The community grew out of the need for students from Serbia in Switzerland to connect with each other, exchange experiences and find answers to practical questions faster.

## Development

Over time, spontaneous gatherings turned into a more organised network: events, partnerships, support for new students and content that remains available to everyone.

## Today

The focus is on quality, professional identity and a sustainable content model that can grow together with the community.
