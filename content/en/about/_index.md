## Who We Are

Serbian Students in Switzerland is a vibrant community dedicated to connecting and supporting Serbian students pursuing education in Switzerland. Founded in 2015, we've grown into a trusted platform with over 120 active members across 5 major Swiss cities.

## Our Mission

To unite Serbian students, alumni and professionals in Switzerland through meaningful events, knowledge sharing and career opportunities, fostering personal growth and professional success.

## Our Values

**Authenticity** — We are genuine, direct and transparent in everything we do.

**Impact** — We focus on creating real value through networking, education and career support.

**Community** — We build lasting connections that extend beyond university years.

**Excellence** — We maintain high standards in organization and execution of all activities.

## What We Do

- **Networking Events** — Professional and social events connecting students, alumni and employers
- **Educational Workshops** — Practical guidance on studying, living and working in Switzerland
- **Career Support** — Internship opportunities, job connections and mentorship
- **Cultural Activities** — Maintaining connection to Serbian heritage while embracing Swiss culture
- **Resource Library** — Guides, advice and practical resources for students

## Join Our Community

Whether you're just starting your studies or well into your career, there's a place for you here. Join hundreds of like-minded individuals building their futures in Switzerland.
